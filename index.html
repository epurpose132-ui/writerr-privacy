WRITERR – Privacy Policy

Developer: RY Studio (Rajat Yadav) · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Summary

WRITERR is a note-taking app. Your notes, PDFs, drawings, timers and settings stay on your device. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and the app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in WRITERR (handwritten notes, imported PDFs and images, typed text, study sessions, schedules and settings) is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Backups you make with Settings → Backup & restore are files that you save and control yourself.

Files and photos

When you import a PDF or add an image, the app only reads the file you pick with Android's file or photo picker. It does not scan your storage or gallery.

Microphone and voice recordings

WRITERR asks for microphone permission only when you start a voice recording in a notebook. Recordings are saved only on your device inside the note they belong to. They are never uploaded, shared or used for any other purpose. You can delete a recording at any time, and you can turn off the microphone permission in Android settings.

Handwriting to text

If you turn on Handwriting to text, WRITERR uses Google ML Kit on your device. The first time, a small language pack is downloaded from Google. Your handwriting is recognised on your device and is never sent to us. ML Kit may send basic diagnostic information (such as device model, app version and performance) to Google; see the ML Kit terms.

Camera (scan to PDF)

When you tap Camera in Scan book pages to PDF, WRITERR opens your phone's own camera app to take a photo of the page. The photo is processed only on your device to make the PDF and is never uploaded. WRITERR does not use the camera at any other time.

Purchases

WRITERR Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether Pro has been purchased on your account. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications and alarms

If you allow it, WRITERR shows local reminders and timer alerts that you set. These are created on your device and are not sent to any server.

Other apps and websites

Quick-open app shortcuts, web links and "search / ask AI" buttons open other apps or your browser. Those apps and websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

WRITERR does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
